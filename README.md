## Market Failure in India’s Agricultural Markets
### ECMA 35530 Final Presentation, University of Chicago Winter 2024
### Bhavya Pandey & Nour Abdelbaki 

This repository contains the code, analysis, and final presentation submitted for the presentation component of the class. 

Our work sought to use microdata from the [SHRUG dataset](https://www.devdatalab.org/shrug) to understand prevailing market failures in India's agricultural markets.
