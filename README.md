## Market Failure in India’s Agricultural Markets
### ECMA 35530 Final Presentation, University of Chicago Winter 2024
### Bhavya Pandey & Nour Abdelbaki 

This repository contains the [code, analysis](https://github.com/bhavyapan/india_agri_project/blob/main/SHRUG_Data_Exploration.ipynb), and final [presentation](https://github.com/bhavyapan/india_agri_project/blob/main/annotated-NotesAdded_ECMA33550_NourBhavya_FinalPPT.pdf) submitted for the presentation component of the class. 

Our work sought to use microdata from the [SHRUG dataset](https://www.devdatalab.org/shrug) to understand prevailing market failures in India's agricultural markets.
